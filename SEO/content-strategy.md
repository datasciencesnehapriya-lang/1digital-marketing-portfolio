# Content Strategy Project

## Project Overview
A practical content marketing project focused on creating engaging,
audience-focused content for local businesses.

## Objective
- Increase brand awareness
- Improve social media engagement
- Attract the right target audience
- Generate potential leads

## Target Audience
Local customers interested in the business's products and services.

## Content Pillars
1. Educational Content
2. Promotional Content
3. Engagement Content
4. Customer-Focused Content
5. Reels & Short-Form Videos

## Platforms
- Instagram
- Facebook
- LinkedIn

## Content Strategy
I create content based on the target audience, business goals,
customer pain points, and platform behavior.

## KPIs
- Reach
- Engagement
- Shares
- Saves
- Profile Visits
- Leads
- Conversion Rate
